# ADR-0007: Test documents are synthetic or verified-public only

- Status: Accepted (founder approved with the Phase 2 plan, 2026-10-29)
- Date: 2026-10-29

## Context
We need realistic documents to build and measure document intelligence. The founder
suggested collecting top firms' documents. Firms' petitions, contracts and case files are
confidential (sigilo profissional) and full of personal data (LGPD), even when the case is
public. Collecting them without consent would create legal risk before launch.

## Decision
1. **Synthetic documents** generated by code (`backend/tests/corpus/generator.py`):
   fictional trabalhista, cível and tributário documents in TJSP style, with invented
   names, valid-format CPF/CNPJ and CNJ numbers, in several variants (digital PDF, scans
   of three qualities, photo, DOCX). The exact text of each page is known, so accuracy
   can be measured.
2. **Public court decisions** only from official sources, and only after checking their
   terms of use (VERIFY SRC-9).
3. **Real documents from pilot firms** only with written consent, anonymized, and after
   a lawyer has reviewed the LGPD basics (REG-1, REG-2).
4. Never collect documents from law firms, court systems or the internet in any other
   way.

## Consequences
- Tests and CI need no real personal data, and anyone can regenerate the corpus.
- Synthetic documents are cleaner and more uniform than real ones. Before launch,
  measurements must be repeated on consented real samples.
